# TechNova Customer Complaints & FAQ

This document contains common customer questions and complaints along with their official solutions. Topics include delivery issues, product defects, returns and refunds, customer service complaints, warranty, payment issues, and order tracking. This file is intended for use as a knowledge base for a customer support RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system.

---

## Delivery Issues

**Q: My order arrived later than the promised delivery date. What can I do?**
Category: Delivery
A: We apologize for the delay. Please share your order number so we can check the shipment status with our courier. If the delay exceeds 3 business days past the estimated date, you are entitled to a 10% discount code on your next order or free expedited shipping on the current one.

**Q: My package shows "delivered" but I never received it.**
Category: Delivery
A: We're sorry for the inconvenience. Please check with neighbors or building reception first, as couriers sometimes leave packages with them. If it's still missing after 24 hours, contact us with your order number and we will open an investigation with the courier and issue a replacement or refund if the package cannot be located.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
Category: Delivery
A: Yes, as long as the order has not yet been dispatched. Contact our support team immediately with your order number and the new address. Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed, but you may be able to redirect it directly with the courier.

**Q: The delivery driver was rude to me. How do I report this?**
Category: Delivery
A: We take this seriously and apologize for the experience. Please provide the order number, approximate delivery time, and a description of what happened. Our logistics team will investigate and follow up with the courier partner, and we will update you on the outcome within 3 business days.

**Q: I wasn't home during delivery — what happens now?**
Category: Delivery
A: The courier will typically attempt delivery up to 2 more times or hold the package at a local pickup point for 3–5 days. You will receive an SMS/WhatsApp notification with next steps. You can also contact us to reschedule a specific delivery time.

**Q: Do you deliver to areas outside Jeddah?**
Category: Delivery
A: Yes, we deliver to most cities across Saudi Arabia. Delivery times outside Jeddah typically range from 2–5 business days depending on the destination. Same-day and next-day delivery are currently only available within Jeddah.

**Q: My order arrived with a damaged box. Is the product inside okay?**
Category: Delivery
A: We recommend inspecting the item before signing for delivery whenever possible. If you've already accepted it, please open it carefully and check the product. If the product itself is damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos of the box and item, and we will arrange a free replacement or refund.

**Q: Can I track my order in real time?**
Category: Delivery
A: Yes, once your order is dispatched you will receive a tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp. You can also check order status anytime by messaging us with your order number.

**Q: I received someone else's order instead of mine.**
Category: Delivery
A: We apologize for this mix-up. Please do not use the item and contact us immediately with your order number and a photo of what you received. We will arrange a pickup of the incorrect item and expedite delivery of your correct order at no extra cost.

**Q: Is same-day delivery available, and how much does it cost?**
Category: Delivery
A: Same-day delivery is available in Jeddah for orders placed before 2:00 PM, for a flat fee of 25 SAR. Orders placed after 2:00 PM will be delivered the next day.

## Product Defects / Quality Issues

**Q: The product I received doesn't turn on / isn't working.**
Category: Product Defects
A: We're sorry to hear that. First, please confirm the device is charged or properly connected to power. If the issue persists, this is covered under warranty — contact us with your order number and a short description or video of the issue, and we will arrange a free repair, replacement, or refund depending on the product condition and warranty terms.

**Q: I received a product with a scratch or cosmetic damage.**
Category: Product Defects
A: We apologize for this. Please send clear photos of the damage along with your order number within 7 days of delivery. If the item is confirmed to have arrived damaged, we will send a free replacement or issue a refund, whichever you prefer.

**Q: The battery on my new phone/laptop drains very quickly.**
Category: Product Defects
A: This can sometimes be resolved with a software update or by disabling battery-heavy background apps. If the battery still drains abnormally fast after these steps, please bring the device (or ship it) to our service center for a free diagnostic check under warranty.

**Q: My product stopped working after a few weeks of normal use.**
Category: Product Defects
A: This is covered under our standard manufacturer warranty. Please provide your order number and a description of the issue. Our technical team will run a diagnostic and, if a manufacturing defect is confirmed, repair or replace the item at no cost.

**Q: The item I received doesn't match the description or specifications online.**
Category: Product Defects
A: We apologize for the discrepancy. Please share the order number along with what you received versus what was listed, and we will investigate. If confirmed, you can choose between a full refund, an exchange for the correct item, or store credit.

**Q: My headphones/earbuds only work in one ear.**
Category: Product Defects
A: Please try re-pairing the earbuds and checking for a software/firmware update via the companion app. If only one side still works after these steps, this is a hardware defect covered by warranty — contact us for a free replacement.

**Q: The screen of my new device has dead pixels or discoloration.**
Category: Product Defects
A: This qualifies as a manufacturing defect. Please send a photo or video showing the issue clearly along with your order number, and we will process a replacement unit at no additional cost.

**Q: My smartwatch stopped tracking my heart rate / steps accurately.**
Category: Product Defects
A: Please ensure the watch is worn snugly and the sensor area is clean, then restart the device. If accuracy issues continue, this may be a sensor defect — contact our support team for a warranty diagnostic and possible replacement.

**Q: The charger that came with my product overheats.**
Category: Product Defects
A: For your safety, please stop using the charger immediately. This is a serious defect — contact us right away with your order number, and we will send a free replacement charger and, if needed, escalate for a broader quality check.

**Q: My printer keeps jamming or printing blank pages.**
Category: Product Defects
A: Please check that the ink/toner cartridges are properly seated and not empty, and that you're using compatible paper. If the issue continues after these checks, this is likely a hardware fault covered under warranty — contact us for a technician visit or replacement.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: How long do I have to return a product?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: You may return most products within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and includes all accessories. Some categories (like opened earphones/earbuds for hygiene reasons) may have different conditions — check the product page or ask us directly.

**Q: I want to return a product because I simply changed my mind. Is that allowed?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Yes, change-of-mind returns are accepted within 14 days as long as the item is unused and in original condition with all packaging and accessories. A small return shipping fee may apply unless the return is due to a defect or our error.

**Q: How do I request a return or refund?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Contact us with your order number and reason for return. We'll issue a return authorization and arrange pickup (for defective/wrong items, pickup is free; for change-of-mind returns, a fee may apply). Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund is processed within 5–7 business days.

**Q: I returned my item due to poor delivery service — will I be refunded in full?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Yes. If a return is caused by a delivery issue on our end (late arrival, wrong item delivered, damaged packaging, etc.), you will receive a full refund including any delivery fees, with no return shipping cost to you.

**Q: How will I receive my refund — card, wallet, or bank transfer?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Refunds are issued back to your original payment method. Card payments typically take 5–10 business days to reflect depending on your bank, while wallet/store credit refunds are usually instant to 24 hours.

**Q: Can I exchange a product for a different size, color, or model instead of a refund?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Yes, exchanges are available subject to stock. Contact us with the order number and the item you'd like instead. If there's a price difference, you'll either pay the difference or receive the difference back.

**Q: My item was on sale/discount — can I still return it?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Yes, discounted items follow the same 14-day return policy as regular-priced items unless explicitly marked as a final sale item on the product page.

**Q: It's been over a week and I still haven't received my refund.**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: We apologize for the delay. Please share your order number and the date the returned item was picked up or received by us, and we will check the refund status and escalate it with our finance team immediately.

**Q: Do I need the original packaging to return an item?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Original packaging is preferred to ensure the product is protected during transit and to qualify for a full refund. If the original box is missing, contact us — we may still accept the return with a partial deduction, depending on the item's condition.

**Q: Can I cancel my order before it's delivered?**
Category: Returns & Refunds
A: Yes, orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as they haven't been dispatched yet. If the order has already shipped, you can refuse delivery or request a return once it arrives.

## Customer Service Complaints

**Q: I've been waiting a long time for a response from customer support.**
Category: Customer Service
A: We apologize for the delay in response. Our standard response time is within 24 hours on WhatsApp and chat. Please share your previous inquiry details or order number so we can prioritize and follow up with you right away.

**Q: A support agent was unhelpful or dismissive with my issue.**
Category: Customer Service
A: We're sorry to hear about this experience — it does not reflect the service standard we aim for. Please share the date, time, and details of the conversation, and we will review it with our team and follow up personally to resolve your original issue.

**Q: I keep getting transferred between departments without a resolution.**
Category: Customer Service
A: We apologize for the runaround. Please share your issue and order number here, and we will personally see it through to resolution instead of transferring you further.

**Q: I was given incorrect information by a support agent.**
Category: Customer Service
A: We're sorry for the confusion this caused. Please let us know what information you were given, and we will correct it and make sure the right outcome is applied to your case, including any adjustment needed as a result of the error.

**Q: How can I file a formal complaint about my experience?**
Category: Customer Service
A: You can submit a formal complaint here via WhatsApp/chat, by email, or through the "Complaints" section of our app. Please include your order number, a description of the issue, and any relevant photos. Complaints are reviewed within 2 business days.

**Q: I'm unhappy with how my previous complaint was handled.**
Category: Customer Service
A: We apologize that the resolution didn't meet your expectations. Please share your original complaint reference number, and we will escalate your case to a senior support specialist for review.

**Q: Is there a way to speak to a human instead of a chatbot?**
Category: Customer Service
A: Yes, at any point you can type "agent" or "human" to be connected with a member of our support team during working hours (Saturday–Thursday, 9 AM–11 PM).

## Warranty

**Q: What warranty comes with your products?**
Category: Warranty
A: Most electronics come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Select flagship products (like the NovaPhone X12 series and NovaBook Pro line) include a 2-year warranty. Warranty terms are listed on each product page.

**Q: Does the warranty cover accidental damage, like a cracked screen from a drop?**
Category: Warranty
A: No, standard warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not accidental damage, water damage from misuse, or unauthorized repairs. Extended protection plans covering accidental damage are available for purchase at checkout for select products.

**Q: How do I claim warranty service?**
Category: Warranty
A: Contact us with your order number or proof of purchase and a description of the issue. Depending on the product, we'll either arrange a mail-in repair, an in-branch drop-off, or a technician visit, all at no cost if the issue is covered under warranty.

**Q: How long does a warranty repair usually take?**
Category: Warranty
A: Standard repairs typically take 5–10 business days depending on parts availability. If a repair will take longer, we'll inform you and offer a temporary replacement device where available.

**Q: Can I get warranty service if I bought the product from a different branch than the one I'm visiting?**
Category: Warranty
A: Yes, your warranty is valid at any TechNova branch nationwide, regardless of where you originally purchased the item, as long as you have your order number or proof of purchase.

**Q: Is the warranty void if I install third-party software or root/jailbreak my device?**
Category: Warranty
A: Software modifications like rooting or jailbreaking may void warranty coverage for software-related issues, but hardware defects unrelated to the modification are typically still covered. Each case is reviewed individually.

## Payment & Billing Issues

**Q: My payment was deducted but the order wasn't confirmed.**
Category: Payment
A: We apologize for the inconvenience. This is usually a temporary bank processing delay. Please share your order attempt details (time, amount, payment method), and we will verify with our payment provider. If the order truly wasn't placed, the amount will be automatically reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days.

**Q: I was charged twice for the same order.**
Category: Payment
A: We're sorry for this error. Please share your order number and both transaction references if available. We will investigate immediately and refund the duplicate charge within 5 business days.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
Category: Payment
A: We accept Mada, Visa/Mastercard credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, STC Pay, and cash on delivery (available for orders under 3,000 SAR).

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
Category: Payment
A: Yes, installment plans are available through Tabby and Tamara at checkout for eligible orders, typically splitting payment into 3 or 4 interest-free installments.

**Q: I have a discount code that isn't working at checkout.**
Category: Payment
A: Please check that the code hasn't expired and that your cart meets the minimum purchase requirement, if any. If it still doesn't apply, share the code and order details with us and we'll manually apply the discount or issue store credit for the difference.

**Q: Can I get a VAT invoice for my purchase?**
Category: Payment
A: Yes, a VAT-compliant invoice is automatically emailed after every purchase and is also available for download from your order history in the app or website.

## Order Status / Tracking

**Q: How do I check the status of my order?**
Category: Order Status
A: You can check your order status anytime by messaging us with your order number, or by visiting the "My Orders" section in the app or website.

**Q: My order has been "processing" for several days with no update.**
Category: Order Status
A: We apologize for the delay. Please share your order number so we can check with our warehouse team directly. Orders typically move to "shipped" status within 1–2 business days; if yours has exceeded this, we will expedite it.

**Q: Can I combine two separate orders into one delivery?**
Category: Order Status
A: If both orders haven't shipped yet, contact us and we'll do our best to combine them into a single delivery to save on shipping. Once either order has shipped, they cannot be combined.

**Q: I ordered multiple items but only received some of them.**
Category: Order Status
A: Some orders are shipped in multiple parcels depending on warehouse stock locations, especially for larger orders. Check your order details for separate tracking numbers — remaining items are usually delivered within 1–3 additional days. If not, contact us with your order number.

**Q: How do I know when my order has shipped?**
Category: Order Status
A: You will automatically receive an SMS and WhatsApp notification with a tracking link as soon as your order is handed to the courier.
